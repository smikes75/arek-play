Amiga games rebuilt with AREK and playable in your browser. Bring your own disk images.

Alien Breed II: The Horror Continues

Team17, 1993. The intro, the title and the first two levels. Needs disks 1 and 2.

Play

Nothing from the games is hosted here. The pages load your disk image in the browser and verify it; it is never uploaded. The games' own code, translated to WebAssembly, is sealed and opens only with the original disk. There is no CPU interpreter and no Kickstart. Each game's About screen tells how it was built.
