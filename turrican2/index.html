    Arrows: move and jump · Z / Ctrl: fire · H: help · M: sound on / off

    Help

    Arrows: move, jump (up) and duck (down).

    Z or Ctrl: fire. Hold it standing or ducking for the lightning beam; the arrows turn it.

    X or space: power lines; together with fire, the smart bomb. Down + space: the gyroscope wheel (fire drops mines, up ends it).

    1 / 2 / 3: the weapons you have collected.

    Gamepad: stick or pad moves, A jumps, X or the right trigger fires, B is space, Y the special attack, LB / RB weapons, Start pause.

    E: Original / Enhanced · F: fullscreen · P: pause · M: sound on / off · H: this help · Esc: close.

    This is the first level (world 1-1) only. Reaching its exit ends the demo.
