# Turrican II: The Final Fight

Amiga, 1991 · Factor 5 · Rainbow Arts.

## What you just played

The first level of a rebuild: the game's own 68000 code rewritten to JavaScript, routine by
routine, with the graphics, maps and music read from your disk. It is not an emulator. The ported
code is sealed on this page and opens only with an accepted disk (so far the [L]EGEND re-release of
disk 1; an original retail disk is not yet verified).

## Measured against the original

- 154 recorded runs of the original in the vAmiga emulator, compared frame by frame in 24
  categories of the game state: 90 % of the 293,154 frames match in every category.
- The picture of world 1-1 matches the emulator's frames in 99 % or more of the pixels.

## Made with

Codex (OpenAI) from September 2026, then Claude (Anthropic) in Claude Code from October 2026, with
the vAmiga emulator as the measuring instrument and the amiga-re-kit toolkit.

Enhanced keeps the game's 50 Hz rules and adds a wider 448 × 256 view, smooth motion at 60 to
144 Hz and a smooth status bar.
